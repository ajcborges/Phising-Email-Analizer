    What to do next

      How the score works

      The score is fully explainable. Every warning sign carries a fixed number of points and is listed with the exact text that triggered it.

      0–29 Low Risk

      Few or no warning signs detected. Stay alert, but nothing obvious stands out.

      30–59 Suspicious

      Some warning signs are present. Verify the sender through an independent channel before acting.

      60–100 High Risk

      Multiple strong warning signs. Treat the email as dangerous and do not interact with it.

      Important: what this tool can and cannot do

      • This is an educational triage tool. It identifies warning signs — it can never definitively determine whether an email is malicious or safe.
      • A low score does not guarantee an email is safe, and a high score is not proof of wrongdoing. Always use your judgement.
      • Never click links or open attachments just to test them. Merely opening a link can expose you to malware or confirm that your address is live.
      • To verify an important message, contact the organisation through its official website or app, or a phone number you already trust — not the contact details inside the email.
      • If you may have already interacted with a suspicious email, change the relevant passwords and tell your IT or security team (or your bank) straight away.
